# Architecture rules

- Store optional exam grade-cutoff notes in `exam_analysis_reports.grade_cutoffs` as nullable free text, and preserve them during AI autofill so manually recorded thresholds are not overwritten.
- Both student exam-paper upload entry points use `prepareExamPaperUpload` before encoding and size validation so photo compression and output MIME/extension stay consistent while PDFs remain unchanged.
- Office registration-task corrections update only the task title, description, and assignee; never infer student-record or teacher-assignment changes from free text, because task notes are not structured enrollment data.
- Individual exam feedback stores its selected method on the analysis; shared validation supports independent item/comment submissions, and parent responses expose only published feedback and parent-safe subject materials to preserve review boundaries.
- Tuition ledger uses student_monthly_fee only from LEDGER_START onward; earlier months join non-canceled billing-month payments to students (including withdrawn), equate fees to recorded sums, and never judge unpaid status or expose manual/billing controls or receivable comparisons. Paginated reads fail visibly to prevent false unpaid states.
- Tuition details and receipt dialogs live as siblings in a shared provider; printing mounts a dedicated body portal so only receipts print and no nested Dialog conflicts occur.
- Receipt numbering is transaction-locked by paid-date year and derived from preserved ledger rows; assigned numbers are immutable and canceled payments are retained to avoid numbering gaps.
- Tuition calendar search, status/source/date filters, and list sorting use pure UI-only helpers over existing monthly ledger status; source and recent filters qualify individual non-cancelled billing-month payments before selecting students and latest-payment sorting, keep review flags and monthly totals intact, share scheduled due dates with the calendar, and never recalculate fees or write records.
- Tuition date comparisons use persisted non-cancelled billing due dates by student and billing month, with current month-clamped student settings only as a labelled fallback; compare date-only UTC values, show split payments individually, and preserve cancellation and review flags to avoid false lateness or completion claims.
- Student monthly fee overrides remain separate from course fee and enrollment history; display applicability follows the authoritative monthly fee function, while pending billing repricing conditionally rechecks status and original amounts to preserve concurrent payments and edits.
- Daily payment summaries are read-only projections of paginated ledger records: keep actual payment dates separate from Seoul-local creation dates, preserve billing-month attribution, and separate recorded, review-needed, and canceled amounts without claiming import-run success or monthly full payment.
- Dashboard inline test editing uses canonical lesson test slots first and distinct legacy results as fallbacks; save only changed test fields through the existing slot RPC or original record, preserve time/assistant metadata and submitted state, and surface failed reads or stale edits instead of treating them as empty results.- Historical imported payments (source 출결버스) carry no receipt number: never offer receipts for unnumbered rows, and read payment history with billing-month-bounded, paginated queries whose summaries cover all filtered non-canceled rows.

- In-app paths that add money to a student-month (manual payment, review-queue assignment) must run the shared overpayment check and await an explicit top-toast confirmation before writing, because monthly fees are fixed; pre-ledger months are exempt.
- Unpaid lists judge due-ness with shared `unpaidDue`/`tuitionLedger` helpers: the 미납 tab groups non-cancelled bills per student-month by effective due date (saved bill due date first) inside a chosen date range and lists payments by actual paid date; the calendar uses the same due-by-today rule and shared upcoming toggle, so default counts agree.
- Withdrawal/leave bill cleanup lives in the `cancel_bills_after_withdrawal` trigger (later-month unpaid bills only; the base-month bill stays for the owner); the ledger keeps inactive students only while a non-cancelled bill remains, so cancelled months never resurface as unpaid.
- Fee changes reach bills only through `apply_fee_change`, called with the authoritative monthly fee after saving; it skips paid/partial, payment-linked, owner-edited and multi-row months so recorded money is never repriced.
- Owner notes live in `billing_schedules.note` and are edited only through `BillNoteCell`/`updateBillNote`; `memo` and `extra_memo` stay system-owned (auto-cancel, collector rules), and owner cancel/restore decisions are appended to `memo`, so collector parsing never sees free text.
- Amount-mismatch review payments (needs_review, no linked bill) are settled only through `accept_payment_amount`, which aborts on missing/ambiguous bills or other same-month payments, so an unlinked transfer never silently reprices or double-settles a month.
